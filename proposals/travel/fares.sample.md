## Changes before departure

Voluntary changes are permitted up to two hours before scheduled departure. A
change fee of EUR 45 per passenger per direction applies, plus any difference in
fare between the original and the new booking. Where the new fare is lower, the
difference is not refunded.

## Changes on the day of travel

Passengers holding a Standard fare may request the earlier or later service on
the same route and same calendar day at the airport, subject to seats being
available at the time of request. No change fee applies to a same-day move made
this way, though the fare difference is still payable.

## Cancellation

Cancellation before departure carries a penalty of 50% of the fare paid.
Government taxes and airport charges are refundable in all cases.

## Name changes

Not permitted. A booking may not be transferred to another passenger.

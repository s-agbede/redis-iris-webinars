## If we cancel or delay your flight

Where a Northwind service is cancelled, or delayed more than three hours, you
may choose a seat on the next available Northwind service at no charge, or a
full refund. This applies regardless of the fare brand held, and overrides the
change and cancellation rules in the fare conditions.

## If you miss a connection

Where both flights were booked in a single Northwind reservation, we will
rebook you onto the next available service at no charge. Where the flights were
booked separately, the second booking is treated as a no-show.

## No-show

A passenger who does not present for a flight and has not cancelled in advance
forfeits the fare. Onward and return segments in the same booking are cancelled
automatically.

## Beyond the standard window

If an item develops a fault, your rights are not limited to the 30-day return
window, and they are not limited to full-price items. A reduced item that is
faulty is treated exactly as a full-price item that is faulty.

## What counts as a fault

A fault is a defect in materials or manufacture: seams that come apart, zips or
fastenings that fail, coatings that delaminate, or a garment that does not
perform as described. Ordinary wear, damage from misuse, and failure to follow
the stated care instructions are not faults.

## Waterproof garments specifically

Where a garment is sold with a stated hydrostatic head rating and taped seams,
water ingress through the seams or the face fabric within the expected life of
the garment is a fault. Water entering through an open vent, an undone zip, or
the cuffs is not.

Reproofing a durable water repellent finish is routine maintenance, not a repair
of a fault. Waxed cotton requires periodic rewaxing and is not sold as a
technical waterproof.

## How to claim

Contact us with your order number and photographs of the fault. We will repair,
replace, or refund. Where a fault appears within six months of delivery we do
not ask you to prove it was present at purchase.

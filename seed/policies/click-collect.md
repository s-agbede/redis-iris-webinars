## How it works

Choose collection at checkout and pick your store. Collection is always free,
with no minimum order value. Most orders are ready within 2 hours for stock held
in that store, or 2 to 3 working days where the item ships in from a
distribution centre.

## Collection window

We hold orders for 10 days from the day they are ready. You receive an email and
an SMS when the parcel is available, and a reminder on day 7. Uncollected orders
are returned to stock and refunded automatically.

## Returning a collected order

Orders collected in store can be returned in store or by post, under the same
windows as any other order. Bring the collection email or your order number.

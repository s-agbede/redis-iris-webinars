## Follow the garment label

The care instruction on each product page and on the garment label takes
precedence over general advice. Washing above the stated temperature, tumble
drying where the label forbids it, or using fabric softener on a technical
fabric can void a fault claim.

## Technical outerwear

Wash waterproof shells with a technical cleaner rather than detergent, which
leaves residues that attract water. Reproof with a wash-in or spray-on treatment
when water stops beading. Do not dry clean unless the label says so.

## Waxed cotton

Do not machine wash or dry clean waxed cotton. Sponge with cold water and rewax
once a year, or more often with heavy use. Rewaxing darkens the fabric.

## Knitwear

Hand wash wool and lambswool in cool water, or use a wool cycle. Dry flat, never
on a hanger. Pilling on lambswool in the first few wears is normal and is not a
fault; remove it with a comb rather than a razor.

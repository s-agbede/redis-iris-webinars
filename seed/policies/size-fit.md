## How our sizes run

Our own-brand garments are cut to the size chart published on each product page
and are true to size unless the product description says otherwise. Where a
product is described as slim, size up if you prefer room to layer. Where it is
described as relaxed or oversized, the garment is already cut generously.

## Petite and tall cuts

Petite cuts reduce sleeve and body length and narrow the shoulder, for a height
of roughly 157cm and under. Tall cuts add length in the sleeve and body. The
chest and waist measurements are unchanged between standard, petite, and tall in
the same nominal size.

If a previous order fitted well through the body but the sleeves were long, a
petite cut in the same nominal size is usually the right change rather than
sizing down.

## Brands other than our own

Third-party brands are cut to their own sizing. We publish the brand's own chart
on each product page and do not adjust it.

## Free size exchanges

Exchanging for a different size in the same item is free, including return
postage, within the standard return window.

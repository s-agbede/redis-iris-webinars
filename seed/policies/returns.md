## Standard return window

You may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items
must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with their original tags attached.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 working days of the
return reaching our warehouse.

## Reduced and sale items

Items bought at a reduced price carry a shorter window: **14 days** from
delivery rather than 30. Reduced items may be exchanged or refunded, but the
refund is for the price actually paid, not the original ticket price.

This shorter window does not apply where an item is faulty. Faulty goods are
covered separately and for longer — see the Faulty goods policy.

## Return postage

Returns are free from within the EU using the prepaid label in your parcel. If
you have lost the label, request a new one from your account. Where a return is
made because the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item, we cover postage in
all cases.

## Items we cannot accept back

Pierced jewellery, and underwear or swimwear where the hygiene seal has been
removed, cannot be returned unless faulty. This does not affect your statutory
rights.

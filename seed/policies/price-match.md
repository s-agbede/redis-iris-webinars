## Price match

If you find the same item, same colour, same size, in stock at a lower price
from another EU retailer, we will match it. Send us the link within 7 days of
your order. We do not match marketplace sellers, auction listings, clearance
stock, or member-only pricing.

## Price drops after you buy

If an item you bought at full price is reduced within 14 days of your order, we
refund the difference on request. This applies once per item and does not apply
to items already bought at a reduced price.

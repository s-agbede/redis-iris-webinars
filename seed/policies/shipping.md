## Options and timings

Standard delivery within the EU takes 3 to 5 working days and is free on orders
over EUR 50. Below that it is EUR 4.95. Express delivery is EUR 9.95 and arrives
in 1 to 2 working days when ordered before 14:00 CET.

## Dispatch

Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a working day are dispatched the same day.
Orders placed later, at weekends, or on public holidays are dispatched the next
working day. You receive a tracking link when the parcel leaves our warehouse.

## Split deliveries

Where items in one order ship from different distribution centres, they may
arrive separately. You are charged one delivery fee regardless of how many
parcels arrive.

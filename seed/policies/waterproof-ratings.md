## What hydrostatic head means

A hydrostatic head rating, given in millimetres, measures how tall a column of
water a fabric can hold before it leaks. Higher is more waterproof. It describes
the fabric only, so a high rating on a garment with untaped seams will still let
water in at the stitching.

## What the numbers mean in practice

Below 5,000mm handles light showers for short periods. Around 5,000 to 10,000mm
is suitable for regular rain and everyday wear. 10,000 to 15,000mm handles
sustained rain and wind. Above 15,000mm is intended for prolonged exposure and
hill use.

## Water-resistant is not waterproof

A water-repellent or water-resistant finish makes water bead and run off a
fabric. It is a surface treatment, it wears off, and it can be renewed. It is
not the same as a waterproof membrane and such garments do not carry a
hydrostatic head rating.

Windproof is a third, separate property. A windbreaker blocks air movement and
may shed a passing shower, but it is not built to keep out rain.

## Breathability

Breathability is measured separately, as grams of water vapour passing through a
square metre of fabric in 24 hours. A waterproof garment with low breathability
will feel damp inside from perspiration even when no rain has entered.

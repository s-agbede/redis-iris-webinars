## Gift receipts

Add a gift receipt at checkout and the parcel ships with no prices shown. The
gift receipt carries an order reference the recipient can use to return or
exchange without contacting the buyer.

## Who gets the refund

A recipient returning a gift receives store credit valid for 24 months. A refund
to the original payment method can only be made to the person who paid, on their
request. Exchanges for a different size or colour are available to either party.

## Extended window for December

Orders placed between 1 November and 24 December may be returned until 31
January. The shorter window on reduced items still applies within that period.

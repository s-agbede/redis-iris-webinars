## Where we ship

We ship to the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Norway in addition to the EU.
Delivery takes 5 to 8 working days and costs EUR 14.95.

## Duties and taxes

Prices shown exclude any import duty or tax due outside the EU. These are
collected by the carrier on delivery and are not refundable by us if you return
the item. Check your own country's thresholds before ordering.

## Returns from outside the EU

Return postage from outside the EU is at your cost, and we do not provide a
prepaid label. The return window is extended to 45 days to allow for transit.
Faulty items are returned at our cost wherever you are.

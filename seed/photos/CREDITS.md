## Mapping notes

- **nikon-d610:** Model reference: D610 body; listing condition may differ.
- **sony-a7r-ii:** Model reference: Sony A7R II body.
- **canon-rf85-f12:** Model reference: RF85mm lens mounted on a camera; camera not included.
- **canon-ef85-f18:** Model reference: Canon EF85mm f/1.8 USM lens.
- **nikon-w300:** Model reference: black W300 shown; this listing is orange.
- **nikon-d5600:** Model reference: D5600 body only; kit lenses and accessories not pictured.
- **canon-rf24-105-f4:** Model reference: Canon RF24–105mm F4L IS USM lens.
- **canon-rf50-f18:** Model reference: RF50mm lens mounted on a camera; camera not included.

- **sony-zv-e10-white:** White ZV-E10 with a silver SEL1650 lens and windscreen. The same model-reference image serves the camera and kit records with separate captions; included items are not independently verified. Original image copied without edits.

Mappings use explicit product IDs, reviewed against product titles. Accessory records that mention a camera model are deliberately not mapped to that camera photograph. Unmapped products display a placeholder. Photos do not enter embeddings, index fields or relevance judgements.
